// 08 — Reflections & Lessons Learned

Reflections

Skills Gained Throughout the Course

This course pushed me beyond textbook security into hands-on application. I came in with a Navy background in discipline and operations — I left with practical skills in threat detection, log analysis, and building automated security pipelines. The biggest growth was learning to think like an attacker while building like a defender.

Challenges I Encountered

Building the Watchmen multi-VM system was the most complex challenge — coordinating five agents across different operating systems, debugging inter-process communication, and making the LLM reasoning engine reliable under real conditions. Every failure taught me something about how real SOC environments break and recover.

How My Understanding of Cybersecurity Has Developed

I started thinking of security as a checklist. I now understand it as a continuous process — one that requires curiosity, documentation, and the ability to adapt when attackers do. The Navy taught me that discipline and process matter under pressure. Cybersecurity taught me that the best defense is understanding the offense.

Areas I'm Continuing to Improve

I'm actively working toward my CompTIA Security+ to formalize my knowledge base. I want to go deeper in SIEM tooling (Splunk, Microsoft Sentinel) and improve my malware analysis skills. Every project I complete reveals a new area to explore — that's what keeps this field interesting.
